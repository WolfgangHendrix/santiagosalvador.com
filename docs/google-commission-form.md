Use the title **Commission Inquiry — Santiago Salvador** and this description:

> Tell me about the work you have in mind. I review each request and reply by email. This form includes an optional reference-image upload; Google sign-in is required to access a form with file uploads. If that is inconvenient, email me instead at salvsantiago@gmail.com.

Questions, in order:

1. **Your name** — short answer, required.
2. **Email address** — short answer with email validation, required.
3. **What kind of project?** — multiple choice, required: Original art or illustration; Custom figure or toy; Game art or design; Music or sound; Retro game label restoration or custom design; Other.
4. **Describe your project** — paragraph, required. Ask for purpose, desired result, and approximate scope.
5. **When do you need it?** — short answer, optional.
6. **Approximate budget** — short answer, optional. Do not require a minimum amount.
7. **Reference link** — short answer, optional.
8. **Reference images** — file upload, optional; allow images only, at most five files, up to 10 MB per file. The total upload limit is 1 GB; increase it in Google Forms if needed.

Settings: publish with **Anyone with the link** as responder access; do not enable **Limit to 1 response**; keep response summaries private; enable email notifications for new responses; and use the confirmation message **“Thanks for reaching out. I’ll review your project and reply by email.”** Test the public responder link in a private browser window and submit one test response, including an image. Google requires respondents to sign in for uploads.
