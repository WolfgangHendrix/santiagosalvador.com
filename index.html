THE ARTIST

Santiago Salvador

I am a creator and arranger of art, music, words, and programs. Based in California, I have spent decades exploring visual art, custom toy and figure customization (including award-winning Masters of the Universe pieces), independent video game development, and audio design.

Whether hand-restoring classic cartridge labels so vintage games stay alive for future generations, composing chiptunes and acoustic scores, or building interactive web experiences for SpaceChakra, I believe in making things with heart and sharing them with the community.
