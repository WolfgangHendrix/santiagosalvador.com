Consolation to the Angels That Slipped Through the Cracks of Time

we share the same light that shines down from heaven above
feel the same warmth that God gives us with love
faith is all I have to go on
alone or together we must be strong
like a tree or a plant we grow towards the sky
branch out in beauty and take part in this life
every moment a breath, every whisper a song
held in the arms of the truth that was here all along.
